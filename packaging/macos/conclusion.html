MoteField is installed

Open your DAW and look for MoteField by Rango Labs in Audio Units or VST3 effects. Rescan plug-ins if it does not appear.

Add MoteField to an audio track, play some audio, and start with the First Light preset.

Save your DAW project to retain your settings and recorded loop. Export a WAV when you want to use the loop outside MoteField.
