MoteField

Rango Labs

Granular textures, glitches, delay, freeze, and phrase loops.

This installer adds the universal VST3 and Audio Unit plugins for all users of this Mac. Requires macOS 11 or later and a compatible audio host.

Quit your DAW before installing. Reopen it afterward and rescan plugins if necessary.

If you previously installed MoteField in your home Library, move that older copy out of the plugin folder to avoid duplicate versions.

Recorded phrase audio is temporary: record the output before closing your project.
