Install MoteField

Rango Labs

This installer puts the Audio Unit (AU) and VST3 plug-ins in the correct folders for all users of this Mac.

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 11 or later. A compatible audio host is required.

Before continuing: save your DAW project and quit your DAW.

Click Continue, then Install. macOS will ask you to authorize installation.

Previously installed a manual-copy beta? Move its MoteField bundles out of your home Library plug-in folders before reopening your DAW, so the older copies do not hide this installation.

Your saved projects, user presets and exported audio are preserved.
